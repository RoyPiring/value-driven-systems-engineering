## What this domain covers

Customer-paid solutions and strategy for the operators of small and growing businesses. Includes day-to-day operations systems, intake, customer flow, and strategic engineering for SMB and trade-services contexts.

**What it isn't.** A claim of operational ownership over multi-tenant production customer systems. A guarantee of revenue outcomes.
